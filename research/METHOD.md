# Research method

## Collection

We collect provider documentation, model guidance, reproducible prompt outputs, maintained pattern catalogs, corpus research, and community reports. Official sources establish documented behavior. Community reports help form hypotheses, but do not become facts through repetition.

## Controlled benchmark

Each supported generator receives the same prompts for a product page, operational app, dense dashboard, editorial page, rewrite, executive summary, and error-state flow. Runs use new projects and default settings. We record date, model, template, visible defaults, and deviations.

## Rule admission

A rule enters the catalog when it is documented by the provider, repeats across benchmark runs, or is corroborated by independent artifacts. Each entry includes a better move because prohibition alone creates a new house style.

## Limits

The catalog describes defaults and recurring patterns. It does not detect authorship. A strong local design system can eliminate many tool-specific tendencies. Model and product updates can make a rule stale, which is why sources are checked weekly and rules retain review dates.

## Update policy

Automation may refresh timestamps, changed page hashes, and repository revisions. It may open a pull request. It may not change editorial guidance or evidence levels without human review.
