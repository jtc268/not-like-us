# ChatGPT writing guide

ChatGPT is trained to be helpful across many contexts. Without a narrow voice brief, it can over-explain, over-organize, and make uncertain material sound settled.

## Common defaults to reject

- Acknowledgment followed by a restatement of the request.
- Headings and bullets for material that would be clearer as two sentences.
- “Delve,” “landscape,” “tapestry,” “pivotal,” “robust,” and “seamless” used without precise meaning.
- Repeated triples, rhetorical questions, contrast slogans, and dramatic fragments.
- Vague attribution and invented quotations.
- Title Case Applied To Every Heading.
- A summary that repeats the answer.
- Em dashes used throughout otherwise plain prose.

## Better moves

- Specify speaker, reader, relationship, situation, and desired next action.
- Supply representative writing from the actual author and say what must remain.
- Ask for a minimum edit, not a rewrite, when the source already works.
- Require sources next to unstable claims and verify them independently.
- Use formatting only when it improves retrieval or comparison.
- Stop after the requested outcome.

## Prompt addition

```text
Answer directly in the speaker's established voice. Preserve useful quirks and uncertainty.
Cut setup, hype, vague attribution, forced triples, contrast slogans, and recap endings.
Use headings only when they help scanning. Do not use em dashes.
```
