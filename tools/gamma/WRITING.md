# Gamma writing guide

Gamma's card system rewards concise blocks. Compression can turn evidence into slogans and make every slide follow the same verbal rhythm.

## Common defaults to reject

- A title plus three bullets on nearly every card.
- Headlines that claim more certainty than the source.
- Sentence fragments written to fit rather than communicate.
- Repeated “Key takeaway” boxes that tell the audience what to think.
- Metrics without units, baselines, dates, or sources.
- A final card with an empty inspirational line.

## Better moves

- Write the claim in a complete sentence before compressing it.
- Keep the qualification beside the number it changes.
- Put source names and dates on the card, not only in hidden notes.
- Use the number of bullets the evidence requires.
- End with a decision, question, or next action only when the talk needs one.
